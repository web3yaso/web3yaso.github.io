CITELY / DEAL DESK03 / 07
The answer

An independently deployed compliance intelligence agent.

A human, company, or AI agent hires Citely by creating an ERC-8183 job.

Citely does not claim to know every regulation. It buys evidence from specialized compliance agents, then compiles the evidence into conditions a wallet can verify.
